# Add persistent elemental equipment and a commanded companion guard

Players can now buy, forge and compare permanent gear, resume a story stage with its original build, and freely engrave weapons and armor with earth, water, fire or wind. Matching both slots grants one resonance. The illustrated workshop shows real stat changes, prices and losses before a transaction. This cumulative draft preserves the recovered 0.4–0.6 history on top of `codex/music-classes-type` (PR #11): 18 items, common Lv.5 / epic Lv.9 caps, family sets, named purchases, boss pity and save v9. Real payments remain disabled.

The new 0.7 iteration adds an active cooperation choice: Q or the touch shield calls Bedivere to a fixed patch of ground. After physically arriving, he protects a 32-unit circle for 2.8 seconds, reduces incoming damage by 35% and grants one +25% normal-hit counter after a successful defense. A 10-second cooldown, finite approach, daze cancellation and strongest-only equipment charge prevent repeated activation or multiplier loops. The first captain publishes its actual attack/recovery state for readable HUD prompts; melee starts windup only with a target in real reach. New generated shield art is integrated with provenance.

Validation and exact source hashes are in `docs/validation/0.7.0`. The full regression suite, targeted guard contracts and real OpenGL normal/small/notched captures are included, with failures and teardown/resource warnings retained. Thirty-two new first-chapter runs cover knight earth/fire across three seeds and two movement policies, plus mage/archer across four elements. A separately instrumented 0.6 baseline shows that movement policy explains the knight completion difference: no-command outcomes match between versions. Indiscriminate reactive guarding increases knight downs despite measurable mitigation; this is explicitly not a proven balance fix or a 9/10 claim.

The seven screenshots stage production objects and physical guard arrival for inspection; they are not natural human playthroughs. No merge, store release, real payment activation, new executable or platform certification is part of this draft. Next work is first-chapter teaching and human validation of guard timing, class routes and encounter choices.
